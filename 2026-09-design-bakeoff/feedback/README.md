# Feedback: reviews of the bake-off designs

Reviewers' feedback on the designs in [`showcase/`](../showcase/). It feeds a single synthesis round, after which the group picks a design to build.

## The synthesis round

There's one round. Its inputs are the designs, their briefs, and all the feedback collected here.

**The goal** is the best replacement for the group's website, within the scope of the original site. It's a landing page that presents the organization and its goals and points people to the meetups. It should be simple, usable and information-forward.

The result draws on one or more of the submissions. It doesn't need something from every one: it can come heavily or entirely from one design, if that's where the feedback leads.

This is still a design phase. Judge each design as a direction, imagining what a finished version would look like, rather than by how finished it is. Assume the next-meetup slot will show the real upcoming meetup, whatever placeholder a design currently shows.

## Shape of a review

The final collection will probably happen in Airtable or Google Forms. Until then, each review is a Markdown file in this folder with the fields below, so all reviews can be imported the same way.

**For each design:**
- **Keep:** what the synthesis should carry forward.
- **Change:** what doesn't work, separating structural problems from bugs that would be fixed anyway.
- **Open question:** the decision the synthesis has to make about this design.

**Overall:**
- **Spectacle vs. content:** where the final site should sit, from a page you read with a striking backdrop to an experience you travel through before reaching the content.
- **Palette and type:** which colors and fonts the final design should use, and from which design.
- **Synthesis picks:** the elements to take into the final design, and which design each comes from. It's fine if most or all come from one.
- **Cross-cutting observations:** patterns across designs, such as ideas two teams share or problems they all have.

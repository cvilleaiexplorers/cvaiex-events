# ChatGPT Images + Claude Code

Jill and Nish started from pictures. In ChatGPT, they turned a discussion of the qualities they wanted into image-generated mood boards, then revised the boards after their own critique. They then built the site in Claude Code with Opus 5.5.

## Designs

- **[Community Bulletin Board](designs/community-bulletin-board/)** ([source](https://github.com/jill-ssc/ai-explorers-hackathon)): the homepage as a community bulletin board. Bold condensed type sits beside hand-drawn marks such as brush underlines, sketched checklists, sticky notes and contour lines, on sections that alternate ink, cream and signal orange. The next-meetup card comes first. See its [process narrative](designs/community-bulletin-board/process-narrative.md), its [design brief](designs/community-bulletin-board/design-brief.md) and the mood boards in [`journey/`](designs/community-bulletin-board/journey/).

The design's `site/` folder is the team's `build/`, with its four font families bundled.

# Design brief: Blue Ridge at Dusk

**Concept.** A single-page site for a monthly, volunteer-run AI meetup, built around one idea: *Blue Ridge at dusk*. The background is a live WebGL particle field shaped like the mountain ridgelines west of town, and an ember-colored sun rests on the far ridge. The spectacle comes from the place, not from "AI" imagery. It avoids the category default of purple neural glows and icon-card grids.

**Audience and priorities.** Active builders and curious newcomers matter equally, and most arrive on a phone. The first screen answers three questions without scrolling: what the group is (name, tagline and a short About list), when it next meets, and how to join. "TBA" is a designed state, not an error. Each state has exactly one primary action: *Check Meetup Page* while TBA, *Join us on Meetup* once a date is posted.

**Visual system.** A blue-black ground with no panels, cards, glass or shadows; the particle field supplies the only depth. *Palette:* void `#070a12` for the ground; mist `#e4eaf6` for primary text and soft `#b8c3d8` for secondary; haze `#8ea7d6` and deep haze `#3b5588`, taken from the field, for every blue, including hairlines at 22% and 42% opacity; and a single warm accent, ember `#ff6a3d` (hover `#ff8a63`), kept for the date, the primary action and the sun. *Type:* one superfamily, Sofia Sans (open source, OFL, self-hosted). **Sofia Sans Extra Condensed** at weights 400–500, always in caps, at poster scale with very tight leading (about 0.8), sets the name, headings and the date. **Sofia Sans** regular, around 18px with 1.6 line height, handles reading. Small 600-weight labels in widely tracked caps (0.18em) name content. Any tall, narrow grotesk paired with its regular-width sibling would give the same effect. Thin hairline rules divide lists in place of containers.

**Layout.** The hero is asymmetric. The name, tagline and About list sit on the left, and the next-meetup block is top-aligned on the right with the date in large type over the weekday. Below the hero, the pace is calm: a large mission statement, activities across a row, a Join section, the sponsor, and a footer where the page ends back at the ridge. On a phone the order is name, date, actions, then About.

**Motion and behavior.** The field stays fixed behind the whole page, drifts along the ridges as you scroll and parts under a cursor or finger. When text overlaps the mountains, the haze thins behind it rather than the text getting a box or the mountains shrinking. The sun looks for open sky and sets below the ridge when there's none. A still frame replaces the animation for reduced-motion users or when WebGL is unavailable.

**Transferable principles.** Ground the visual idea in something real and local to the audience. Give your one accent color a meaning, and keep it off everything else. Design the empty or unknown states first. Offer one action per state, not the same link three times. Self-host fonts, and ship a small static build that runs offline.

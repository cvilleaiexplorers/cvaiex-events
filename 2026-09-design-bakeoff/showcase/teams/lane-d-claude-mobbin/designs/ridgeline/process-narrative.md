# Process narrative: Ridgeline

---

## Organizers' notes, 2026-10-26

**Tool.** The team used Claude Code with the Mobbin MCP server connected, so the agent could pull real-world site examples, and no design skill. Everything was written as plain, self-contained HTML pages with inline CSS and JavaScript, with no framework or build step. They worked inside a clone of the group's own site repo, in the kit's `design-hackathon/concepts/` folder.

**Three concepts first.** The team produced three directions and a small hub page linking them:
- **A. Ridgeline:** "based on the Atlas reference." Blue Ridge layers at dusk behind a staggered, wide, uppercase headline and a survey grid.
- **B. Field Map:** a printed topographic map of Charlottesville with the Rivanna River, a pin at Studio IX, and a trail log of past meetups.
- **C. Polaris at 38°:** celestial navigation. A sextant measures Polaris at Charlottesville's latitude, and the past meetups form a constellation.

Each concept has the same TBA and dated next-meetup states, and you can preview either one by adding `?date=2026-12-01` to the address.

**Developing Ridgeline.** They picked Ridgeline and built it up in a few rounds:
- **The hero:** ridge layers, a sun, coordinates and a survey grid, with the content below.
- **One scene:** scrolling descends from Humpback Rocks to downtown, from dusk into night, and drives the sky, ridges, haze, stars, town lights and an altimeter.
- **Indoors:** the descent ends inside. A white wall rises over the night for the content sections, and the sponsor gets a warm oak room.
- **Photos:** placeholder meetup photos, finally set as soft-edged backgrounds that drift and fade in.

The finished page is `site/`. The earlier rounds and the other two concepts are in [`journey/`](journey/).

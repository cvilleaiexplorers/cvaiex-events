# Process narrative: Ridgeline

---

## Organizers' notes, 2026-10-26

**Tool.** The team used Claude Code with the Mobbin MCP server connected, so the agent could pull real-world site examples, and no design skill. Everything was written as plain, self-contained HTML pages with inline CSS and JavaScript, with no framework or build step. They worked inside a clone of the group's own site repo, in the kit's `design-hackathon/concepts/` folder.

**Three concepts first.** In about ten minutes the team produced three directions and a small hub page linking them:
- **A. Ridgeline:** "based on the Atlas reference." Blue Ridge layers at dusk behind a staggered, wide, uppercase headline and a survey grid.
- **B. Field Map:** a printed topographic map of Charlottesville with the Rivanna River, a pin at Studio IX, and a trail log of past meetups.
- **C. Polaris at 38°:** celestial navigation. A sextant measures Polaris at Charlottesville's latitude, and the past meetups form a constellation.

Each concept has the same TBA and dated next-meetup states, and you can preview either one by adding `?date=2026-12-01` to the address.

**Ridgeline, four passes.** They picked Ridgeline and reworked it three more times in about half an hour:
- **v1 (19:15):** a hero of ridge layers, sun, coordinates and survey grid, with the content below it.
- **v2 (19:28):** the page became one scene. Scrolling descends from Humpback Rocks to downtown, from dusk into night, and drives the sky, ridges, haze, stars, town lights and a new altimeter.
- **v3 (19:44):** the descent ends indoors. A white wall rises over the night for the content sections, and the sponsor gets a warm oak room. Framed placeholder photos of meetups arrive here. This is the version they presented.
- **Final (19:46):** two minutes later, the photos lost their frames and became soft-edged backgrounds that drift and fade in, using a set of CC0 placeholder photos.

**What's in the handoff.** The final page is filed as `site/`. v1, v2, v3, the other two concepts and the hub page are in [`journey/`](journey/). The photos v3 points to weren't in the handoff, so v3 shows empty frames where they were.

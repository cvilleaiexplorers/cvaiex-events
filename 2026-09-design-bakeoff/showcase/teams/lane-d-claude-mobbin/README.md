# Claude Code + Mobbin

John's team worked in Claude Code with the Mobbin MCP server connected for real-world references, and no design skill. They built three concepts side by side as single HTML pages, then took one of them, Ridgeline, through three more versions during the session. Ridgeline v3 is what they presented.

## Designs

- **[Ridgeline](designs/ridgeline/):** the homepage is one scroll-driven descent, from the Blue Ridge crest at Humpback Rocks (3,080 ft) at dusk, down through the night to the lights of Charlottesville (594 ft), and then indoors to the white walls, oak and concrete where the meetup happens. An altimeter in the margin counts down the feet. See its [process narrative](designs/ridgeline/process-narrative.md), its [design brief](designs/ridgeline/design-brief.md), and the earlier versions and the other two concepts in [`journey/`](designs/ridgeline/journey/).

Ridgeline and [Blue Ridge at Dusk](../lane-c-claude-impeccable/designs/blue-ridge-at-dusk/) arrived at the same scene, Blue Ridge layers at dusk, from different teams and tools.

The design's `site/` folder is the team's final Ridgeline page with its photos.

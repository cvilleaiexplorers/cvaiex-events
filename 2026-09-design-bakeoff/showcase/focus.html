How they got there

  1. Public research on the groupSifan had Codex research the community from public sources. The direction came from a local reference: the Ix site's textile-mill history.
  2. Generated artwork, hand-coded siteCodex generated the filament-knot artwork, strands woven into one form, and wrote the site in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no framework.
  3. Cut copy, next meetup liveSifan's direction was to cut repetitive copy, focus on what a visitor needs and pull the next meetup straight from Meetup.

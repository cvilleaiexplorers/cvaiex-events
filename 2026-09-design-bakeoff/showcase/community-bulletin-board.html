Community Bulletin Board

The homepage as a community bulletin board: bold condensed type beside hand-drawn marks, on sections that alternate ink, cream and signal orange. The next-meetup card comes first.

Led by
Jill H. and Nish T.
Made with
ChatGPT Images, Claude Code
Source
jill-ssc/ai-explorers-hackathon
Open the site ↗ See the journey →

How they got there

  1. Qualities into a mood boardJill and Nish listed the qualities they wanted (curious, open, member-driven, rooted in Charlottesville) and had ChatGPT turn them into an image-generated mood board.
  2. Critique: too touristy, not techy enoughThe first board leaned toward local atmosphere and underplayed a group exploring new technology. They kept the people with laptops and pushed for contrast, heavier type and visible AI and coding cues.
  3. Bolder board, built in Claude CodeThe revised board set the character, and they built the site in Claude Code, trading photos of people for hand-drawn marks.

Type
Four faces with clear jobs: Barlow Condensed for big statements, Fraunces for headings, Barlow for body text and Caveat for handwritten notes.
Hand-drawn marks
Brush underlines, sketched checkboxes, a curved arrow, sticky notes, contour lines and a dot grid, used as accents against a strict type system.
First screen
The group name and a one-line welcome beside a “Next meetup” card with the date, venue and an RSVP button, or “TBA: Check Meetup Page.”
History
Every past meetup as a linked card, newest first, under a “since March 2025” headline and two stats.

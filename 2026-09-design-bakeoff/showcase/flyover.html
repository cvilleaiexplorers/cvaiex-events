Flyover

The CvAIE logo becomes the ground. Rebuilt from tiles over real elevation, it carries you down across a ridge and over Charlottesville's actual buildings as you scroll, and the content lands as four cards at the end.

Led by

How they got there

  1. Three concepts, harsh critiqueEugene prompted Claude Code directly, with no design skill. The brief asked for three genuinely different concepts, a harsh critique pass and a phone-width check.
  2. Fly along the logoThe concept he took: “the logo is the water; you dive and fly along it.”
  3. Real terrain and city, all scroll-drivenThe water became real USGS elevation, Charlottesville's buildings came over from Cosmic City, and all self-running motion was removed, so a still scroll position is a still frame.

The logo as land
About 193,000 tiles carry the logo's colors at each spot, laid over a real ten-mile square of Albemarle County.
The flight
About seven and a half screens of scrolling with nothing else on screen: dive, skim the ground, climb Carter Mountain, then drop toward downtown.
Content deck
Four cards fly in and settle in a row over downtown: Our Mission, What We Do, Join Us and Get Involved.
Measured color
The orange was taken from the logo, where 90% of the orange pixels share one value.

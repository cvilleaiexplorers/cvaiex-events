Blue Ridge at Dusk

A live particle field shaped like the ridgelines west of Charlottesville, in haze blue, with one ember accent kept for the next-meetup date, the Join buttons and a setting sun.

Led by
Mike P.
Made with
Claude Code, Impeccable

How they got there

  1. Interview and product briefImpeccable scanned the repo and interviewed the team, then wrote a product brief: two equal audiences, active builders and curious newcomers, plus the anti-goals and the content rules.
  2. Seven concepts, one WebGL challengerIt drew seven concepts from the group's own world, rolled dice to pick a lead and dealt outside styles beside it. The team found the literal concepts contrived and took the WebGL Shader Portal challenger, grounded locally as “Blue Ridge at dusk.”
  3. Room feedback moved About above the foldScreenshots, a design linter and a reviewer subagent tightened the build over several rounds. Then people in the room asked for About Us on the first screen and one action per state, which gave the clearest wins.

Type
Sofia Sans Extra Condensed, in caps at poster scale, for the name, headings and the date; regular Sofia Sans for reading.
Motion
The field drifts along the ridges as you scroll and parts under a cursor or finger. Where text meets the mountains, the haze thins behind it.
First screen
Name, tagline and About on the left; the next-meetup date at hero scale on the right. On a phone: name, date, actions, then About.
Next meetup
“TBA” is a designed state with one action, Check Meetup Page. Once a date is posted, the action becomes Join us on Meetup.

Cosmic City

Charlottesville's real buildings float under a live star field. You pick a venue, fly there, and walk inside a stylized community room or along the Downtown Mall.

How they got there

  1. Public map and building dataTravis worked in OpenCode, an open-source coding agent, and imported the City of Charlottesville's building footprints and streets and a real star catalog.
  2. Staged preview-and-feedback roundsEach stage ended in a preview and a feedback round. The green palette and a star sky kept as “a subtle background Easter egg” both came out of those rounds.
  3. Fly over, then walk the MallThe finished explorer orbits the city, flies to each venue and walks the Mall, with ordinary crawlable pages behind it. Every event, venue and photo records where it came from.

Navigation
Real places are the menu: pick Studio IX, UVA or Kardinal Hall and the camera flies there, or take a five-stop guided tour that pauses when you step in.
Discovery dock
A search box (“Find a place, a meetup, a spark of inspiration…”) with a microphone for voice search.
Type
Space Grotesk for headings, DM Sans for body text and small tracked monospace capitals for labels and coordinates.
Sources on everything
Each event carries the date it was checked, each venue says whether its pin is approximate, and the site says plainly where its coverage is incomplete.
